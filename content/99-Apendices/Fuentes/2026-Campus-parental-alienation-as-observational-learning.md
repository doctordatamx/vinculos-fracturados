## Resumen

Fuente tipificada como [on_topic]. Síntesis del abstract/contenido disponible: This paper proposes a social-learning reading of parental alienation, drawing on Bandura’s social-learning theory to frame the phenomenon as an observational-learning process—exposure, internalization, reproduction, and reinforcement. It introduces the Model of Relational Experimentation (MRE) and presents descriptive findings from the first national survey on parental alienation in Italy, conducted in 2016 by the association F.L.A.Ge, in which 100 self-identified alienated parents (53% mothers, 47% fathers) completed an 11-item self-report questionnaire.
