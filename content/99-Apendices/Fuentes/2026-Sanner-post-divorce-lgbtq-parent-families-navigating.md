## Resumen

Fuente tipificada como [on_topic]. Síntesis del abstract/contenido disponible: ABSTRACT Objective The purpose of this study was to investigate underexplored dynamics of post‐divorce LGBTQ‐parent families. Background In LGBTQ‐parent families in which children were conceived in the context of a prior different‐gender partnership, children experience the coinciding events of parental separation and a parent's coming out as LGBTQ. In recent decades, research on LGBTQ‐parent families formed following different‐gender divorce has stalled, perhaps due to the increasing emphasis on planned LGBTQ‐parent families, or families formed after parents have come out.
