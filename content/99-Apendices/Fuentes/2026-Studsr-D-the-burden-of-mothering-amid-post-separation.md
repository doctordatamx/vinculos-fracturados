## Resumen

Fuente tipificada como [psychom, on_topic]. Síntesis del abstract/contenido disponible: Separation or divorce might escalate intimate partner violence. This study contributes to emerging research on the lived experiences of mothers dealing with post-separation abuse. The aims are (1) to explore what situational and relational tasks mothers are navigating in the context of partner abuse, and based on the findings, (2) to discuss how their experiences are related to social norms of mothering. To develop rich data, open-ended interviews were conducted with eight Norwegian mothers.
