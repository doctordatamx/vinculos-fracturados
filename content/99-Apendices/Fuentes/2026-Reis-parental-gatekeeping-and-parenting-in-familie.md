Síntesis del abstract/contenido disponible: Parental gatekeeping plays an important role in interparental dynamics, yet its broader associations with parenting remain underexplored, as most studies have focused primarily on its links with father involvement. Adopting a systemic perspective, we employed network analysis to investigate the interrelations between parental gatekeeping and multiple dimensions of parenting.
