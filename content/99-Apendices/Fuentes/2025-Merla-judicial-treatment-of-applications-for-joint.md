## Resumen

Fuente tipificada como [on_topic]. Síntesis del abstract/contenido disponible: Abstract Joint physical custody (JPC) of children after parental separation is increasingly prevalent in many countries. The literature describes a series of criteria that are—or should be—considered by judges who must rule on applications for JPC. However, little is known about how family courts evaluate these criteria, or about the normative representations that shape their evaluations. Based on the perspectives of lawyers and judges in Belgian family courts—collected in 2018 through an online survey and semi-structured interviews—this article examines the most commonly used criteria, how these criteria are evaluated, and the underlying normative representations of the family.
