## Resumen

Fuente tipificada como [on_topic]. Síntesis del abstract/contenido disponible: The present piece will analyse the judgment of May 27, 2019, issued by the Court number 9 of Valladolid, which focuses on the legal regime applicable to pets. It will examine whether a pet should be treated in accordance with the Civil Code legal status provisions (i.e. movable property or chattel) or another legal regime should be applicable, although it differs from that strictly provided by our legal system and recognizes the sentience of pets.
