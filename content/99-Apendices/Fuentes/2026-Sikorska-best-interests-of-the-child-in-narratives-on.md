## Resumen

Fuente tipificada como [on_topic]. Síntesis del abstract/contenido disponible: This study examines how the Best Interests of the Child (BIC) are constructed in the narratives of Joint Physical Custody (JPC) proponents (father activists), doubters (mother activists), and decision-makers (judges). Focusing on Poland, it explores how these narratives reflect the recognition of child agency after parental separation. This study is theoretically informed by the Narrative Policy Framework (NPF) and by the concept of BIC. It draws on 53 court rulings, 16 citizen petitions, and transcripts from five parliamentary sessions as well as 5 in-depth interviews (IDIs) with father activists, 4 with mother activists, and 8 with judges.
